# Requirements — Nueva Ruta Ops

Status: approved by human (2/10/2026 12:56 p.m. COL)
Normative terms: MUST and SHALL are acceptance obligations; SHOULD is a preferred behavior.

## A. Lead agent and handoff

### REQ-A01 — Idempotent ingestion

The system SHALL accept simulated CTWA and organic inbound events through a documented webhook or queue contract. A repeated source event ID MUST return the existing result without duplicating leads, messages, acknowledgements, drafts or transfers.

### REQ-A02 — Lead fields

Every lead SHALL preserve: ID, inbound timestamp, channel, source detail, creator when known, original message, fictional phone, initial status, consent context and conversation-window timestamps.

### REQ-A03 — Classification

Every actionable message SHALL receive exactly one decision: `respond`, `ignore` or `escalate_human`. The decision SHALL record timestamp, reason code, explanation, rule version, AI metadata when used, and a link to its draft or task.

### REQ-A04 — Minimal pre-qualification

For `respond`, the system SHALL extract or request only:

- approximate debt amount or range;
- general debt type;
- state of residence;
- preferred language;
- preferred contact time;
- desire to speak with a consejero.

It SHALL NOT request SSN, full account numbers, credentials, detailed creditors, income, expenses, rates, fees or credit score.

### REQ-A05 — Fictional debt policy

- Unknown amount: create a safe question draft.
- Below USD 5,000: information/general review, not an automated rejection.
- USD 5,000–100,000: continue pre-qualification if other rules allow it.
- Above USD 100,000: human escalation.
- Invalid or contradictory amount: human escalation.
- Credit card, medical and unsecured personal loan: may continue.
- Mortgage, auto, taxes, business, legal and student-loan questions: human escalation.

All values are editable fictional policies and SHALL be labeled as such.

### REQ-A06 — Fictional state coverage

California, Texas and Florida SHALL be seeded as supported. Other or unknown states SHALL create coverage review and remain visible in the funnel.

### REQ-A07 — Human draft gate

Any response containing debt guidance, program detail, eligibility, savings, personal recommendation or follow-up SHALL remain a draft until approved by an authorized person.

### REQ-A08 — Automatic transactional messages

Only these fixed versioned templates MAY be automatically sent:

1. receipt acknowledgement including the warning not to share SSN, full accounts or credentials;
2. after-hours acknowledgement;
3. opt-out confirmation.

They MUST NOT be generated by AI, MUST be idempotent, MUST respect opt-out and MUST be globally disableable.

### REQ-A09 — Escalation

Ambiguity, risky language, data sensitivity, low AI confidence, unsupported coverage, legal threats and configured handoff words SHALL create an owned task with reason, priority, redacted summary, due time and suggested role.

### REQ-A10 — Human-approved transfer

Only an authorized operator MAY initiate partner transfer. The system SHALL never infer approval from pre-qualification or disposition text alone.

## B. CRM automation

### REQ-B01 — Supported dispositions

The system SHALL support exactly:

- `No Answer`
- `Info Sent`
- `Transferido`
- `Call Back`
- `No le interesa`

Unknown dispositions SHALL be rejected and logged as actionable errors.

### REQ-B02 — Stage changes

Disposition mapping SHALL be:

| Disposition | Commercial stage | Required data |
|---|---|---|
| No Answer | `contact_attempted` | usable phone or documented missing-phone task |
| Info Sent | `info_sent` | approved substantive message or recorded external action |
| Transferido | `transferred` | explicit operator approval and partner request ID |
| Call Back | `callback_scheduled` | future callback timestamp and timezone |
| No le interesa | `closed_not_interested` | reason; opt-out evidence when explicit |

### REQ-B03 — Audit trail

Every disposition SHALL record actor, timestamp, prior stage, resulting stage, reason, generated draft, rule/template version and side-effect status.

### REQ-B04 — Outbox and retries

External effects SHALL be written transactionally to an outbox. Retryable failures SHALL follow configurable backoff. Exhausted failures SHALL enter a dead-letter queue with manual replay. Successful effects SHALL not repeat.

### REQ-B05 — Required error cases

The UI SHALL expose recovery for webhook failure, missing phone, invalid disposition, exhausted retry and manual replay.

## C. Data and reporting

### REQ-C01 — Data layers

Partner data SHALL move through `raw → normalized → canonical → reconciliation → reporting`. Raw rows SHALL remain immutable and traceable by file, row and checksum.

### REQ-C02 — Cleaning

Cleaning SHALL document phone normalization, timestamps, creator IDs, duplicates, invalid/future/ambiguous dates and canonical record selection.

### REQ-C03 — Authority boundaries

- Nueva Ruta owns original lead origin and creator attribution.
- Consejería Clara owns whether an enrollment was reported.
- Reconciliation owns the evidence-based link between them.
- Human review owns ambiguous resolution.

### REQ-C04 — Matching

Matching order SHALL be:

1. exact shared external ID when present;
2. unique exact normalized phone plus compatible evidence;
3. review case for contradiction, ambiguity or multiple candidates.

The system SHALL NOT overwrite either source during reconciliation.

### REQ-C05 — Funnel

The reporting funnel SHALL distinguish received lead, pre-qualified lead, approved transfer, accepted partner webhook, partner-reported enrollment and reconciled enrollment.

### REQ-C06 — Stalled work

Views SHALL show age, threshold, owner and next action for stale commercial stages, escalations and partner deliveries.

### REQ-C07 — Attribution and commission proxy

Reports SHALL expose null creator IDs, phone mismatches, duplicates, ambiguous links and unmatched enrollments. Only a conflict-free reconciled enrollment MAY enter the non-monetary `potentially_commissionable` count.

### REQ-C08 — Traceability

A reviewer SHALL be able to trace a reported enrollment from dashboard metric to reconciliation decision, canonical record and raw row.

## D. Content system

### REQ-D01 — Creator profiles

The product SHALL contain five clearly fictional complete creator profiles with ID, name, handle, platforms, audience archetype, voice, content pillars, CTA style, attribution parameters and compliance notes.

### REQ-D02 — Content sources

The product SHALL contain ten fictional lead questions, objections or invented trends with source ID, type, date, channel, theme and explicit fictional provenance.

### REQ-D03 — Prioritization

Source ranking SHALL expose frequency, funnel proximity, freshness and compliance-risk factors rather than returning an unexplained AI score.

### REQ-D04 — Scripts

The product SHALL deliver three prioritized Spanish scripts for 30–45 second vertical video. Each SHALL link to a source and remain human-reviewable. Conditional savings language and all compliance controls apply.

## E. Cross-cutting requirements

### REQ-E01 — Rule governance

Rules SHALL be editable outside source code. YAML provides reproducible defaults; the database stores runtime drafts and immutable published versions. Publication requires supervisor role and diff review. Decisions retain the exact version used.

### REQ-E02 — Role access

Seeded password-protected accounts SHALL exist for operator, supervisor and Influgain analyst. The analyst SHALL not receive unnecessary unredacted chat access.

### REQ-E03 — PII redaction

Likely SSN, full account data and credentials SHALL be redacted before ordinary UI, logs and AI calls and SHALL trigger supervisor escalation.

### REQ-E04 — AI fallback

The local product SHALL operate without an AI key. Every decision SHALL expose whether AI was intentionally skipped, used successfully, or replaced by deterministic fallback. Failures SHALL distinguish configuration, transport, provider, output-validation and compliance layers with normalized reason codes. Provider timeout, invalid schema, low confidence or blocked output SHALL preserve ingestion and route uncertain work to a person.

### REQ-E05 — Local reproducibility

From documented prerequisites, one wrapper around Supabase CLI and Docker Compose plus migration/seed/import commands SHALL make the demo usable in less than fifteen minutes without a paid dependency.

### REQ-E06 — Synthetic-only fixtures

Bundled leads, phones, creators, enrollments and content SHALL be clearly fictitious. No real consumer data is permitted.

### REQ-E07 — Scale evidence

A reproducible load test SHALL submit at least 200 inbound events and report accepted/rejected events, throughput, p50/p95 latency, backlog drain, losses and duplicate effects. Claims SHALL distinguish deterministic stub results from live-provider results.

### REQ-E08 — Demo reset

A supervisor SHALL have a protected, confirmed reset that restores the synthetic baseline deterministically and audits the action.

### REQ-E09 — Documentation and video

The repository SHALL deliver a product-facing README, exported n8n workflows, architecture, decisions, compliance, cleaning, AI-use and scale notes. The application flow SHALL support a concise evaluator-led walkthrough.

### REQ-E10 — External services

Real external accounts or temporary hosting SHALL require separate authorization. A hosted demo SHALL contain only synthetic data, use password protection and secrets outside the repository, and have a documented shutdown date.

### REQ-E11 — Production-like application boundary

The local stack SHALL use Next.js App Router for the Spanish interface and Supabase local for PostgreSQL and authentication. FastAPI SHALL be the only API allowed to read or mutate business-domain data. Next.js and n8n SHALL use documented FastAPI contracts rather than write domain tables directly. Supabase migrations SHALL be the sole schema authority.
